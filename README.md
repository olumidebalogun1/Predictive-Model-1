## **I. Key Business Question**

### **What will our monthly sales look like over the next two years, based on historical purchasing patterns**?

## **II. Project Overview**

### **1. Business Challenge**:
The business needs to plan and make informed strategic decisions for inventory, marketing, staffing, and budgeting. However, uncertainty around future sales trends makes it difficult to allocate resources effectively and identify growth opportunities.

### **2. Project Goal**:

To forecast **monthly sales over the next two years** using a **Linear Regression model** trained on historical purchasing patterns. This will enable proactive business planning based on expected revenue trends.

## **III. Approach**:

-	**Data Cleaning**: Clean and standardize the dataset to remove errors, ensure consistency, and establish a reliable foundation for accurate analysis.

-	**Feature Expansion**: Enrich the dataset by adding supplementary columns that provide additional context and enhance the depth of analysis.

-	**Data Aggregation**: Aggregate historical sales data by month and year.

-	**Feature Engineering**: Create a time-based index (Time) to serve as an independent variable in the regression model.

-	**Model Selection**: Use **Linear Regression** to capture the linear trend in monthly sales.

-	**Forecasting**: Predict sales for the next 24 months using the trained model.

-	**Visualization**: Plot both historical and forecasted sales to visualize trends and support decision-making.
